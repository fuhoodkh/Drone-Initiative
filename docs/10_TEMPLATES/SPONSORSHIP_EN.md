## 1) One-page overview

### Headline
In-person summer drone program in Muscat for grade 9–11 students, teaching flight, maintenance, and FPV build in a safe, supervised environment.

### Why sponsor?
- **Direct community impact:** equipping school students with future technical skills.  
- **Strong corporate image:** supporting innovation and the knowledge economy in Oman.  
- **Brand visibility and naming rights:** depending on the sponsorship tier.  

### Target audience
Grade 9–11 students, parents, schools, partners, tech community, and media.

### Key outcomes
Hands-on training hours, FPV build projects, accredited certificates, and a final **Demo Day** event.
