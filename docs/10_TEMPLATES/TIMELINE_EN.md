## 1) Purpose

## 2) Phases

### Phase A — Preparation (4–8 weeks before start)
- Confirm partners and approvals  
- Approve budget and sponsorship plan  
- Secure venue and safety plan  
- Finalize curriculum and equipment list  
- Open registration  

### Phase B — Pre-launch (1–2 weeks)
- Shortlist and accept students  
- Assign cohorts and schedule  
- Prepare venue and equipment  
- Team training on safety procedures  

### Phase C — Delivery (3–5 weeks)
- Weekly delivery + weekly status report  
- Monitoring progress + continuous improvement  

### Phase D — Closing (1 week)
- Demo Day + certificate ceremony  
- Final program and sponsor report  
- Equipment inventory and maintenance
