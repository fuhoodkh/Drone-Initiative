## Slide 1 — Title
- Drone Initiative
- Summer Drone Program (Oman)
- Tagline: Modern, safe, future-ready technical education

## Slide 2 — The problem
- Schools need applied tech pathways
- Youth interest is high but practical programs are limited
- Drones require safety + structured learning, not ad-hoc hobby activity

## Slide 3 — The solution
- 3–5 week, in-person program in Muscat
- Four tracks: theory, flight, maintenance, FPV build
- Certified supervision + safety-first execution

- Cohort-based small groups
- Expansion: other governorates after pilot

## Slide 5 — Program outcomes (measurable)

- Built FPV drones + tested flights
- Certificates + demo day showcase
