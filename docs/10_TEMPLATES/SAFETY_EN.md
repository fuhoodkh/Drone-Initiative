# Safety & Risk Management Plan — Summer Drone Program

**Version:** v0.1 (draft)  
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Owner:** Safety Officer + Program Management  

> This document must be approved before any practical training starts.

---

## 1) Purpose
To ensure a safe training environment for students, staff, and visitors, and to comply with the requirements of the host venue and relevant authorities.

---

## 2) Scope
- Classroom/theoretical sessions  
- Practical flight training (flight zones)  
- Maintenance workshops  
- FPV build workshops (soldering/tools)  
- Final event (Demo Day)  

---

## 3) Roles and responsibilities
- **Safety Officer:** approves zones and procedures, manages incidents  
- **Trainer/Supervisor:** performs pre-activity checks and direct supervision  
- **Logistics team:** sets up barriers, signage, and equipment  
- **Students:** follow instructions and do not use equipment unsupervised  

---

## 4) Operating zones (zoning)
Define a site map with:
- Flight Zone  
- Staging/Preparation Area  
- Audience/Visitor Area (no entry to flight zone)  
- First aid / emergency point  

---

## 5) Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### 5.1 Pre-flight
- Inspect airframe and propellers  
- Check battery condition and secure mounting  
- Validate control settings  
- Define permitted path and altitude  
- Confirm no persons are in the flight zone  

### 5.2 During flight
- Supervisor-to-student ratio as per policy  
- Immediate stop in case of unsafe behaviour or malfunction  

### 5.3 Post-flight
- Disconnect and safely store batteries  
- Log notes/issues  
- Store aircraft and equipment securely  

---

## 6) FPV workshop safety (soldering/tools)
- Eye protection during soldering  
- Adequate ventilation  
- No unsupervised tool use  
- First aid kit + suitable fire extinguisher in place  

---

## 7) Risk register (template)
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drone collision | Medium | High | Zones + barriers + progressive training | Safety Officer |
| Soldering burns | Low | Medium | PPE + supervision + ventilation | Workshop lead |
| Battery (LiPo) issues | Low | High | Safe storage/charging + LiPo bag | Technician |

---

## 8) Incident management and reporting
- Definitions of “incident” and “near-miss”  
- Incident report form (time/place/description/action)  
- Guardian notification protocol when needed  
- Weekly review for preventive actions  

---

## 9) Approvals and requirements
- Host venue requirements  
- Regulatory requirements (if applicable)  
- Photo/privacy policy integration
