# Curriculum & Schedule — Summer Drone Program

**Version:** v0.1 (draft)  
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Owner:** Training team + Program management  

---

## 1) Overview
- **Duration:** 3–5 weeks  
- **Target group:** Grade 9–11 students  
- **Outcomes:** Flight skills, maintenance basics, FPV build, certificate, final showcase  

---

## 2) Learning outcomes
By the end of the program, students should be able to:
- Apply core air-safety and operating-zone concepts  
- Perform basic take-off/landing and controlled manoeuvres on training tracks  
- Diagnose common issues and perform basic repairs  
- Assemble an FPV drone and configure basic transmission/control  
- Work effectively in a team and document their work  

---

## 3) Tracks
- **Track 1:** Theory (principles, types, safety)  
- **Track 2:** Flight training (progressive skills)  
- **Track 3:** Maintenance (components, inspection, diagnostics)  
- **Track 4:** FPV build (assembly, soldering, configuration)  

---

## 4) Weekly plan (example)

### Week 1 — Fundamentals & Safety
- Theory: flight principles, drone types, safety rules  
- Practical: basic control, take-off/landing on stations  
- Assessment: safety test + basic skills checklist  

### Week 2 — Flight skills & tracks
- Practical: training tracks, precision, simple emergency procedures  
- Maintenance: routine checks, batteries, props, controllers  
- Assessment: track performance + simple maintenance task  

### Week 3 — Advanced maintenance & FPV intro
- Maintenance: fault diagnosis, basic part replacement  
- FPV: components overview, build planning  
- Assessment: diagnosis task + build plan  

### Week 4 — FPV build & testing
- Assembly, soldering, initial configuration and ground tests  
- Progressive FPV flight under supervision  
- Assessment: functioning FPV setup + safety checklist  

### Week 5 (optional) — Project & Demo Day
- Team project (challenge/mission) + documentation  
- Final Demo Day + certificates
