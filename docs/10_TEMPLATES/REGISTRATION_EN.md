## 4) Registration steps (flow)
1) Student/guardian fills in the online registration form  
2) Required documents are uploaded  
3) Initial review by the registration team  
4) Conditional acceptance + fee/confirmation (if applicable)  
5) Parent/guardian signs consent and media policy  
6) Student is assigned to a cohort and receives the schedule  

> **Platform note:** These steps appear as a **guide** at the top of the Registration section, with clear **Download** (forms) and **Upload** (signed copies) buttons.
