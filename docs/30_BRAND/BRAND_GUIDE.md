# Brand Guide — Drone Initiative (DI)

## Brand positioning
**Modern, safe, future-ready technical education** for youth in Oman, focused on drones (flight, maintenance, and FPV build).

## Tone of voice
- Clear and confident (avoid hype)
- Safety-first and compliant
- Youth-friendly but professional
- Impact-driven (skills, outcomes, future jobs)

---

## Visual identity

### Logo
- Primary logo: `docs/30_BRAND/logo/di-logo-primary.svg`
- Icon mark: `docs/30_BRAND/logo/di-mark.svg`

Rules:
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the “DI” letters.
- Do not distort or recolor outside the palette.

---

## Color palette

### Primary
- **Midnight**: `#0B1220` (headings, nav)
- **Sky**: `#2D7FF9` (primary actions, links)

### Secondary
- **Aqua**: `#15B8A6` (highlights, success)
- **Sand**: `#F5F1E8` (background, warm neutral)

### Neutrals
- **Ink**: `#111827`
- **Slate**: `#334155`
- **Gray**: `#94A3B8`
- **Mist**: `#E5E7EB`
- **White**: `#FFFFFF`

Accessibility:
- Prefer Sky on Midnight or White on Midnight.
- Body text should be Ink on White/Sand.

---

## Typography

Arabic:
- Recommended: **Cairo** or **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**

English:
- Recommended: **Inter** or **IBM Plex Sans**

Hierarchy (documents / platform):
- H1: 28–34px / bold
- H2: 20–24px / semibold
- Body: 14–16px / regular
- Caption: 12–13px / regular

---

## Components (document + platform)

Buttons:
- Primary: Sky background, White text
- Secondary: White background, Sky border/text

Cards:
- White on Sand background, subtle shadow, 12px radius

Tables:
- Header row: Midnight background, White text
- Zebra rows: Mist / White

---

## Document layout (print)
- Page size: A4
- Margins: 18–22mm
- Header: logo left (EN) / right (AR), doc title opposite side
- Footer: version + date + page number
