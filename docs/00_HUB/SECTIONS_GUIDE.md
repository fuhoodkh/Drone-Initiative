# Sections Guide (What to write + why)

This guide defines **every major section** in the initiative and how the team should write it. Each section here maps 1:1 to a section in the Platform UI.

---

## 1) Initiative Proposal (`PROPOSAL`)

### Purpose
Secure stakeholder alignment and approvals (government / education / aviation / venue).

### Audience
Decision makers, partners, program owners, sponsors (high-level).

### What “good” looks like
Clear problem → solution → plan → cost → outcomes. Short, credible, measurable.

### Team inputs
- Program brief (Arabic text)
- Partners list
- Timeline (3–5 weeks)
- Venue + safety plan assumptions
- Budget ranges

### Writing guide
- Start with a 1-page executive summary
- Use bullets and tables more than paragraphs
- Keep objectives measurable (skills, hours, outputs, certificates)

---

## 2) Sponsorship Pack (`SPONSORSHIP`)

### Purpose
Convert sponsors with a clear value exchange.

### Audience
CSR leads, marketing directors, business owners, government funds.

### What “good” looks like
Strong benefits, clear packages, audience reach, activation plan, deliverables.

### Team inputs
- Sponsor tiers and pricing
- Brand placement inventory
- Media plan
- KPI plan

### Writing guide
- Lead with impact (students, skills, Oman innovation)
- Show tier table + what each tier gets
- Include “activation ideas” for sponsors (booths, demo day, branding)

---

## 3) Registration Pack (`REGISTRATION`)

### Purpose
Standardize how students apply, how we select, and how we manage consent/safety.

### Audience
Students, parents/guardians, schools, program admin team.

### What “good” looks like
Clear steps, clear eligibility, clear dates, clear documents required.

### Team inputs
- Eligibility (grade 9–11)
- Capacity per cohort
- Selection criteria
- Policies (photo consent, safety, medical)

### Writing guide
- Provide a simple flow chart (steps + dates)
- Use plain language; avoid jargon
- Include FAQs

---

## 4) Investment Pitch Deck (`INVEST_DECK`)

### Purpose
Raise investment or strategic funding by showing scale potential.

### Audience
Investors, strategic partners, innovation funds.

### What “good” looks like
Story + market + model + traction plan + ask. Visually strong.

### Team inputs
- Unit economics (cost per student)
- Pricing/fees (if any) + sponsorship model
- Expansion plan (other governorates)
- Partner commitments

### Writing guide
- 10–14 slides max
- One message per slide
- Use charts, not dense text

---

## 5) Program Curriculum & Schedule (`CURRICULUM`)

### Purpose
Translate goals into a teachable plan (3–5 weeks).

### Audience
Trainers, operations team, partners (education/aviation).

### What “good” looks like
Weekly outcomes, daily plan, lab requirements, assessment.

### Team inputs
- 4 program tracks: theory, flight, maintenance, FPV build
- Trainer availability
- Facilities

### Writing guide
- Define learning outcomes per week
- Include required equipment per module
- Include assessment rubric

---

## 6) Operations Plan (`OPS`)

### Purpose
Execute reliably: people, process, procurement, vendors, logistics.

### Audience
Program management, logistics, vendors.

### What “good” looks like
Runbook-style: who does what, when, and how we handle incidents.

### Writing guide
- Include RACI (roles)
- Include checklists (daily opening/closing, equipment)
- Include escalation paths

---

## 7) Safety & Risk Plan (`SAFETY`)

### Purpose
Protect students, staff, and partners; meet aviation/venue requirements.

### Audience
Civil aviation, venue, trainers, guardians.

### What “good” looks like
Risk register + SOPs + emergency plan + compliance mapping.

### Writing guide
- Be specific (zones, barriers, PPE, pre-flight checklist)
- Include incident reporting process

---

## 8) Budget & Financial Model (`BUDGET`)

### Purpose
Make funding decisions and control costs.

### Audience
Program owner, finance, sponsors, partners.

### What “good” looks like
Transparent assumptions, line items, scenarios, break-even (if applicable).

### Writing guide
- One table for summary, one for detail
- Always show assumptions beside totals

---

## 9) Partner MoUs & Letters (`MOU`)

### Purpose
Formalize partner commitments and responsibilities.

### Audience
Partners legal/admin.

### What “good” looks like
Clear scope, term, roles, branding, liability, data/privacy.

---

## 10) Media Kit (`MEDIAKIT`)

### Purpose
Enable consistent communications (press, social, partners).

### Audience
Marketing team, partners, media.

### What “good” looks like
Key messages, boilerplate, visuals, usage rules.
